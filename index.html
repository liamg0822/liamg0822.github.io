Five Star Channels

Five Star Channels is a private tool that runs a small group of Telegram news channels owned by one person. It writes posts, turns them into short vertical videos and, with the account owner's permission, uploads those videos to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts. The owner reviews every draft in the TikTok app and decides whether to publish it.

The tool is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's TikTok account.
