Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

What we receive from TikTok

When you connect a TikTok account, TikTok gives the Service an access token, a refresh token, your account's open ID and display name. The Service asks only for the permissions user.info.basic and video.upload.

How it is used

The tokens are used only to upload videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts and to show which account is connected. The Service does not read your videos, followers, messages or any other data, and does not sell or share data with anyone.

Where it is stored

Tokens are stored in a file on the Service's private server, readable only by the Service. Videos are kept on the server for up to 30 days and then deleted.

Deleting your data

Disconnecting the account in the Telegram bot deletes its tokens from the server immediately. You can also revoke access in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services.
