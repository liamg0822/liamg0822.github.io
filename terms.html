What the service does

Five Star Channels (the "Service") is a private content tool used by its owner to manage their own Telegram channels and their own TikTok accounts. When an account is connected, the Service uploads short videos to that TikTok account's inbox as drafts. Nothing is published on TikTok without the account owner finishing the post in the TikTok app.

Who may use it

Only the owner of the Service and the TikTok accounts they connect. Connecting an account means you own it or are allowed to manage it.

Content

Videos are made from the owner's own channel posts and freely licensed images, credited in the caption. The account owner is responsible for what they publish and must follow TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.

Disconnecting

You can disconnect at any time in the Service's Telegram bot ("Disconnect TikTok") or in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services.

The Service is provided as is, without warranties. It may stop or change at any time.
